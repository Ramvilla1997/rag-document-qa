Remote Work Policy. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's home office time zone, when everyone must be available for meetings. Employees who work outside their home country need written approval from People Operations at least 30 days in advance, because tax and payroll rules differ by location. Company laptops must use full disk encryption and the VPN when connecting to internal systems. Home office stipend: the company reimburses up to 500 dollars per year for ergonomic equipment such as a chair, monitor, or keyboard. Receipts must be submitted through the expense portal within 60 days of purchase.
